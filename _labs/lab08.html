Attendance

This week's lab is just a survey. To get credit, you need to attend lab and submit the survey password to Gradescope (described below). Students in mega lab only need to submit the password.

Required Questions

Fill out the Mid-Semester Survey.

After you submit the survey, you will be shown a password. Copy it down: you need it to submit this lab.

Submit Assignment

This lab has no starter files, so there is nothing to download, and you don't need VS Code or Provenance to submit it.

  2. Upload lab08.txt to the Lab 8 assignment on Gradescope before the deadline. Late submissions receive no credit.
