Q2: Cucumber

Cucumber is a card game. Cards are positive integers (no suits). A Cucumber game has a number of players, numbered 0 through players - 1: 0, 1, 2, and 3 in a 4-player game.

A Round is created with its starter player and the Cucumber game it is played in. In each Round, the players each play one card, starting with the starter and in ascending order (player 0 follows player 3 in a 4-player game). If the card played is as high as or higher than the highest card played so far, the player who played it takes control. Once every player has played one card, the round is complete, and its winner is the last player who took control. If a player tries to play out of turn or after the round is over, the card is not played, and play returns an error message instead, which play_round prints.

Complete the Round class, adding instance attributes as needed.
